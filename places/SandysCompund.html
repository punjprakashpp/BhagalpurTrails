Sandy's Compound (Jai Prakash Udyan )

  1. Home
  2. Sandy's Compound

Overview

Sandy's Compound is a 30-year-old sports complex located in Bhagalpur City, Bihar, opposite the police line in Bhagalpur. It is a multifaceted recreational area that includes various sports facilities and a charming natural setting, making it one of the best fields in Bhagalpur.

History

The land for Sandy's Compound was acquired by the government in 1906 for public purposes. It was named after a Briton who served as the Mayor of Bhagalpur at the time. The acquisition of this piece of land, which measures approximately 307 bighas, 15 cottahs, and 14 chitaks, was documented in the Calcutta Gazette on July 18, 1906.

Sports Facilities

  • Doordarshan TV Centre: Located within the compound, this center serves as a significant media hub in the region.
  • Volleyball Court: A well-maintained court for volleyball enthusiasts.
  • Small Open Stadium: Suitable for hosting various sports events and activities.
  • State Level Matches: The complex hosts state-level matches, making it a crucial venue for sports in Bhagalpur.
  • Tennis and Basketball Courts: State-of-the-art facilities for tennis and basketball players.
  • Cricket Stadium: Although small, this stadium has historical significance as it hosted a Ranji Trophy match in 1981/82 where the Bihar cricket team defeated the Assam cricket team by an innings and 2 runs.


Recreational Facilities

  • Cafeteria: Offers delightful snacks and beverages for visitors.
  • Open-Air Theatre: Hosts captivating events in a scenic outdoor setting.
  • Swimming Pool: Provides a refreshing experience for swimmers.
  • Yoga Center: A free facility for wellness seekers to practice yoga in a peaceful environment.
  • Gym: A fully-equipped gym for fitness enthusiasts.
  • Toy Train: Offers joyful rides around the park, adding a fun element for families and children.
  • Playgrounds: Multiple playgrounds for children to enjoy various activities.

Location

Sandy's Compound is strategically located and bounded by prominent roads:

  • North: Cleveland Road
  • South: Central Jail Road
  • East: Central Jail Road
  • West: Beatson Road

The complex is nestled in the heart of Bhagalpur, surrounded by the villages of Adampur, Kalibari, and Khunjurpur.

Significance

Sandy's Compound is not only a hub for sports and recreational activities but also a place that blends natural beauty with modern amenities. It serves as a vital community space in Bhagalpur, offering diverse activities for people of all ages. Whether you're a sports enthusiast, a fitness seeker, or someone looking to relax in a beautiful setting, Sandy's Compound provides the perfect environment.
