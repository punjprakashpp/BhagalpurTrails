Ghanta Ghar (Deep Narayan Singh Smarak)

  1. Home
  2. Ghanta Ghar

Overview

Bhagalpur Ghanta Ghar, also known as the Clock Tower, is a significant landmark situated in the heart of Bhagalpur city in the Indian state of Bihar. This iconic structure, standing tall and illuminated at night, is surrounded by vendor stalls, vibrant markets, and bustling bazaars, making it a central hub of activity and commerce.

Historical Significance

Constructed during the British colonial period in the late 19th century, the Bhagalpur Ghanta Ghar was dedicated to King Edward VII of Britain. The clock tower's design showcases a blend of colonial and indigenous architectural styles, characterized by its imposing height, intricate carvings, and ornate embellishments. Over the years, the Ghanta Ghar has become an enduring symbol of Bhagalpur's rich history and cultural heritage.

Architectural Features

  • Height: 25 feet
  • Design: A blend of colonial and indigenous styles
  • Clock: Meticulously maintained, functional timepiece

The tower stands as a functional and aesthetic monument, with its clock still accurately marking the passage of time. The chimes of the clock echo through the streets, adding a unique charm to the city's ambiance.

Surroundings

The area around the Ghanta Ghar is bustling with activity, featuring:

  • Markets and Bazaars: Offering handicrafts, textiles, fruits, flowers, clothes, shoes, snacks, and drinks.
  • Quaint Cafes: Providing a place for relaxation and socializing.
  • Hanuman Mandir: A famous temple located nearby.
  • Nursery: Close to the tower, adding greenery to the surroundings.


Cultural Importance

The Ghanta Ghar holds a special place in the hearts of Bhagalpur's residents. It serves as a gathering spot for celebrations, rallies, and community events, reflecting the resilience and spirit of the people. The tower has been a witness to numerous historical and cultural events in the city.

Preservation Efforts

Both government and non-government organizations, including the Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage (INTACH), are actively involved in preserving this historic monument. Recent renovation work has added a fountain to enhance the aesthetic appeal of the tower.

Visiting Information

  • Open Hours: 24 hours, seven days a week
  • Visiting Charges: None
  • Best Time to Visit: Early or late evening to avoid traffic and enjoy the view during sunset

Valuable

The Ghanta Ghar Clock Tower is more than just a landmark; it is the soul of Bhagalpur. Its historical significance, architectural beauty, and central location make it a must-visit for both locals and tourists. The tower stands as a timeless symbol of Bhagalpur's identity and pride, continuing to attract visitors with its charm and history.
